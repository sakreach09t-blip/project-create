02 — The Craft

Composed on location. Never in a lab alone.

  1. 01

    Sourced

    Our perfumer travels to the place itself and works with local growers and distillers to source raw material within the same season it's harvested.

  2. 02

    Recorded

    Field notes are taken on-site — humidity, stone, rain, smoke — the ambient character no formula sheet can supply from a desk.

  3. 03

    Distilled

    Extraction happens close to the source, keeping transport and time-to-distillation as short as the terrain allows.

  4. 04

    Blended in Paris

    Five origin extracts return to our Paris atelier, where the final accord is set, tested, and bottled by hand in small batches.

03 — The Journal

A perfume should tell you where you are, not just who you are.

Most fragrance is built around a feeling — confidence, seduction, memory. We started from somewhere else: a place. A specific latitude and longitude, with weather, geology, and a season attached to it.

Meridian keeps a running field journal from every trip — the raw notes our perfumer takes before a single accord is mixed. It's the part of the process most houses don't show you.
